Fontkit 2.0.4 Mac OS encoding data attribution

Fontkit's src/encodings.js contains eight single-byte mappings
corresponding to the Apple vendor tables hosted by the Unicode Consortium.
For each table, entries 0x80 through 0xFF match the referenced data
except at 0xCA. Fontkit maps that byte to U+0020 in place of U+00A0.

The Croatian, Greek, Icelandic, Central European, Romanian, and Turkish
tables carry this copyright notice:
(c) 1995-2002, 2005 by Apple Computer, Inc., all rights reserved.

https://www.unicode.org/Public/MAPPINGS/VENDORS/APPLE/CROATIAN.TXT
https://www.unicode.org/Public/MAPPINGS/VENDORS/APPLE/GREEK.TXT
https://www.unicode.org/Public/MAPPINGS/VENDORS/APPLE/ICELAND.TXT
https://www.unicode.org/Public/MAPPINGS/VENDORS/APPLE/CENTEURO.TXT
https://www.unicode.org/Public/MAPPINGS/VENDORS/APPLE/ROMANIAN.TXT
https://www.unicode.org/Public/MAPPINGS/VENDORS/APPLE/TURKISH.TXT

The Gaelic and Inuit tables credit Michael Everson of Everson Typography
for developing the character sets.

https://www.unicode.org/Public/MAPPINGS/VENDORS/APPLE/GAELIC.TXT
https://www.unicode.org/Public/MAPPINGS/VENDORS/APPLE/INUIT.TXT
