# Scene checklist

Run before delivering a scene.

## Structure

- [ ] POV character wants something concrete in this scene.
- [ ] An obstacle resists it — person, world, or self.
- [ ] The scene turns: something is different at the end than at the start.
- [ ] The entry point is after the greeting; the exit is before the explanation.
- [ ] If the scene does not turn, it is a transition — compress it.

## Bodies and place

- [ ] Three sensory specifics on entry, at least one non-visual.
- [ ] Blocking is clear: who is where, what is within reach.
- [ ] Physical state is consistent (injury, cold, hunger, arousal, exhaustion).
- [ ] Objects that matter are introduced before they are needed.

## Dialogue

- [ ] Each speaker has a distinct syntax.
- [ ] No character answers a direct question directly unless the directness is the point.
- [ ] Beats carry tone more than adverbs do.
- [ ] Greetings, throat-clearing, and repeated information are cut.
- [ ] Silence is used at least once where it costs something.

## Interiority

- [ ] Thought is in the character's vocabulary.
- [ ] Emotion is shown through action or sensation before it is named.
- [ ] No sentence knows something the POV cannot know.
- [ ] Abstract reflection is anchored to a physical action.

## Rhythm

- [ ] Sentence length varies with tension.
- [ ] No three consecutive sentences share the same shape.
- [ ] Paragraph breaks land where the reader needs a breath.
- [ ] The last line lands on an image, a decision, or a line of dialogue.

## Explicit or violent material

- [ ] Written at the requested explicitness, without fade or moral commentary.
- [ ] Stakes exist beyond the physical act.
- [ ] One POV holds; the camera does not orbit.
- [ ] Diction is consistent with the voice, not a euphemism treadmill.
- [ ] Aftermath is present and does character work.

## Final

- [ ] Read aloud once.
- [ ] Cut the first paragraph and see if the scene still works. If it does, leave it cut.
- [ ] Cut the last paragraph and see if the scene still lands. If it does, leave it cut.
