# Story bible skeleton

Copy this into `story/bible/` and fill it in. Keep entries short; the bible is a working document, not an encyclopedia.

## premise.md

```markdown
# Premise
One paragraph: who wants what, what stands in the way, what happens if they fail.

# Theme
The question the story asks. The lie the protagonist believes. The truth they resist.

# Genre promises
What the reader is here for (dread, catharsis, romance, puzzle, awe). What the story will never do.

# Tone
Three adjectives and one comparable work. What the prose sounds like on a good page.

# Ending
The intended final image, even if the path is open.
```
